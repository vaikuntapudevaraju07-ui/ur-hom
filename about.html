Our Heritage

UR HOM was conceived with a singular aspiration: to introduce world-class interior architecture to the culturally opulent city of Rajahmundry and coastal Andhra Pradesh.

From expansive riverfront estates along the Godavari to bespoke modern duplexes and statement corporate headquarters, our multi-disciplinary studio harmonizes master interior architects, lighting scenographers, and generational artisan craftsmen.
